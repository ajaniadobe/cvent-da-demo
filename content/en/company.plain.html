Meet Cvent CEO & Founder, Reggie Aggarwal

The Cvent business idea first came to light after Reggie, a lawyer by trade, spent years painstakingly planning hundreds of events for a non-profit of which he was president. He knew there had to be a better way than yellow sticky notes, spreadsheets, and emails.

In 1999, Reggie found the "aspirin" to this pain point: Cvent. Since then, we've passionately helped our customers not only manage their meetings and events, but make them more impactful.
