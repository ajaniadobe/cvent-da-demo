Cvent Community

Your first stop for support

The Cvent Community is your first stop to find answers, learn our products, and connect with other users. From there, you can:

  • Get 24/7 chat support with your personal virtual assistant. Login required.
  • Explore the Knowledge Base. Browse an extensive library of how-to-articles, FAQs, videos, and more.
  • Access training courses to improve your understanding of key product features. Login required.
  • Ask fellow Cvent users. Post a question to the seasoned planners and hoteliers that make up our community. Login required.
  • Review open or past cases. Look up the solution to a question you’ve asked us before. Login required.
