Frequently asked questions

What types of partners does Cvent work with?
Cvent partners with a broad range of organizations—including third-party service providers, independent software vendors (ISVs), value-added resellers (VARs), meeting and event agencies, and travel management companies. Whether you're offering tech, services, or strategy, there's a place for you in our Partner ecosystem.
How do I join the Cvent Partner Program?
Joining is simple. Just complete our online Partner Application and our team will follow up with next steps based on your business model and areas of interest.
Is there a cost to join the program?
It depends on the type of partnership. Many of our partner types have no upfront cost, while others—like services or technology partners — may involve a small additional investment to ensure you have the resources and training to succeed. We’ll guide you based on the best fit for your business.
Do you offer training or certifications?
Yes! We provide robust training and certification options through Cvent Academy for Partners, covering everything from platform fundamentals to advanced usage. Partners also gain access to sales enablement tools designed specifically for agencies and resellers through Cvent’s Partner Portal.
Can I earn commissions or referrals through the program?
Yes! Qualified partners can earn referral incentives or commissions based on the program track and type of opportunity. Specific terms are outlined during onboarding.
Can I list myself as an official ‘Cvent Partner’ on my website?
Absolutely—once you're onboarded and in good standing, you’ll receive official Cvent Partner branding guidelines and a badge to showcase your partnership.
Does Cvent provide partners with leads?
In some cases, yes. While lead sharing is typically reserved for highly engaged or top-tier partners, we do provide all partners with the opportunity to promote themselves in our marketplaces, and with badges and press kits to promote their status.
